There are many different types of enemies you may encounter:
Enemy Mods (Currently the visual indication of these may be tough to identify):
- Blinky (Teleports on hit)
- Bobster (Spawns enemies)
- Flower (Has petals that must be shot in order to shoot the center)
- Goldy (Extra Score)
- Pulse (Scales in size up and down)
- Slime (Splits like a slime)
- Snorlax (After damage will spawn healing targets around it, if they aren’t destroyed the main body will heal)

Enemies are comprised of enemy mods. Mini-Bosses, and Bosses will contain these mods. As you defeat more bosses the base amount of mods on enemies changes. On level 0 the Mini-Bosses have 1 mod, and the Boss has 2 mods. After defeating the boss, all enemies on the next level will have 1 mod by default, with Mini-Bosses and Bosses now having an extra mod as well. 

If you shoot an enemy in the center, you will get a crit. On some enemies you will be able to easily identify the "crit area."

You can locate shops and buy different attachment to buff you. (As of Alpha 1.0 the enemy scalability is not strong and item balance is non-existent)
